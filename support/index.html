Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a previous purchase on a new device?

Sign in to the new device with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe, install Bodhi Tree, and go to the You tab → Premium → Already subscribed? Restore purchases. Apple will return your active entitlement in a few seconds.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Bodhi Tree → Cancel Subscription. You can also tap "Manage Subscription" inside the app, which deep-links to the same screen. Cancelling stops the next renewal but you keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

If you cancel before the 7-day free trial ends, you are not charged.

I was charged twice / charged after cancelling — what do I do?

Apple, not Bodhi Tree, processes all subscription billing. Apple is the only party that can issue a refund. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the Bodhi Tree purchase, and request a refund. If Apple's automated flow doesn't help, reply to your App Store receipt email — that opens a support thread with Apple. We unfortunately cannot refund payments on Apple's behalf.

How do I switch the app between English and Traditional Chinese?

Inside Bodhi Tree: You → Settings → Language. Or in iOS: Settings → Bodhi Tree → Language.

My meditation sessions aren't showing up in Apple Health

Bodhi Tree only writes a Mindful Session if you've granted permission. To check or change this:

  1. Open iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Bodhi Tree.
  2. Make sure Mindful Sessions is turned on under "Allow Bodhi Tree to write data".
  3. Complete a session of at least one minute. The session appears in Apple Health → Browse → Mindfulness within a few seconds.

Bodhi Tree never reads from Apple Health and only ever writes Mindful Minutes for sessions you complete inside the app.

Notifications stopped working

  1. Open iOS Settings → Notifications → Bodhi Tree and confirm "Allow Notifications" is on.
  2. Inside Bodhi Tree, go to You → Reminders and check that the bells you want are switched on.
  3. Notifications are scheduled locally by iOS — if your iPhone is in Focus or Sleep mode, scheduled bells may be delivered silently or held until the Focus ends.

iCloud sync isn't working

  1. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud on every device (iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud).
  2. Inside Bodhi Tree, You → Sync across devices must be on for each device.
  3. iCloud key-value sync can take a few minutes to propagate. Closing and reopening the app, or backgrounding for ~60 seconds, usually triggers a sync.
  4. If a device can't reach iCloud (no Wi-Fi, signed-out account), Bodhi Tree continues to work normally with on-device data and will sync the next time iCloud is reachable.

How does the free tier compare to Premium?

The free tier includes:

  • The daily teaching
  • A meditation timer with three tuned bells (a singing bowl, a temple bell, and a zen bowl) and an optional warm-up
  • A starter selection of sutras
  • The Buddhist calendar with observance alerts
  • Up to 20 bookmarks
  • Bodhi Tree widgets

Premium adds:

  • The full sutra library
  • Advanced study tools
  • Ambient meditation sounds and interval bells
  • Unlimited bookmarks
  • Journal history with full-text search and customization
  • Personalized teaching selection
  • Practice statistics over time

Premium is $6.99/month or $39.99/year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. You can cancel at any time.

What is the "Dana scholarship"?

Dana is the Buddhist practice of generosity. If cost is a barrier, you can request a free one-year Premium scholarship from inside the app — no questions asked. Open You → Premium → Receive Dana and follow the prompts. The grant is local to your device.

What does Bodhi Tree do with my data?

Nothing. We do not have a server that receives your data. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising trackers, and no third-party telemetry. Reflections, bookmarks, journal entries, and meditation history are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are mirrored to your own iCloud key-value store, managed by Apple — we never see them.

The full details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Because Bodhi Tree stores everything on your device:

  • To delete your reflections, bookmarks, or journal entries, do so inside the app.
  • To delete everything, uninstall the app. iOS will purge the app's sandbox.
  • To remove iCloud-synced copies, iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and find Bodhi Tree, or simply uninstall on every device that had sync on.

What iOS versions are supported?

iOS 17.0 and later. Bodhi Tree is iPhone-only at this time.

I found a bug / I have a feature request

We'd love to hear it. Email support@bodhitree.app with what you saw and what you expected. Screenshots are very welcome.

Where do the sutra translations come from?

The bundled translations are public-domain English renderings or our own translations from public-domain source texts. Specific translator credits are listed inside each sutra's "About this translation" panel.

I want to send feedback that isn't a support request
