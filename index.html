Bodhi Tree for iOS

A quiet place for Buddhist practice.

Bodhi Tree brings together a daily teaching, a meditation timer, a private journal, and a sutra library on iPhone. No accounts. No tracking. No feed.

Privacy Policy Support

What Is Inside

Designed for newcomers and longtime practitioners across Theravada, Zen, Tibetan, Pure Land, and broader Mahayana traditions. Available in English and Traditional Chinese.

  • Today
    A daily teaching chosen for the season and your tradition.
  • Sutras
    A searchable library including the Heart Sutra, Diamond Sutra, Dhammapada, and key suttas from the Pali Canon.
  • Meditate
    A clean timer with tuned bells, optional warm-up, interval bells, and a breath ring.
  • Journal
    A private, on-device space for end-of-session reflections.
  • Mantras and Glossary
    Mantras with transliteration, plus concise Pali, Sanskrit, and Chinese definitions.
  • Buddhist Calendar
    Vesak, Bodhi Day, Ulambana, and optional gentle observance reminders.

Privacy by Design

Bodhi Tree ships no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or trackers. Practice data stays on your device unless you opt in to iCloud sync through your own Apple account.
