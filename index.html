A quiet place for practice.

Bodhi Tree is a Buddhist study and meditation app for iPhone. A daily teaching, a meditation timer, and the great sutras — without accounts, without tracking, without a feed.

Privacy Policy Support

What's inside

Designed for newcomers and longtime practitioners across Theravada, Zen, Tibetan, Pure Land, and broader Mahayana traditions. Available in English and Traditional Chinese.

  • Today — a daily teaching chosen for the season and your tradition.
  • Sutras — a searchable library including the Heart Sutra, Diamond Sutra, Dhammapada, and key suttas from the Pali Canon.
  • Meditate — a clean timer with three tuned bells, an optional warm-up, interval bells, and a breath ring. Lotus Breath and Loving-Kindness practices included.
  • Journal — a private, on-device space for end-of-session reflections.
  • Mantras & Glossary — recorded mantras with transliteration, plus concise Pali / Sanskrit / Chinese definitions.
  • Buddhist Calendar — Vesak, Bodhi Day, Ulambana, with optional gentle alerts.

Privacy by design

Bodhi Tree ships no third-party analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no trackers. Everything you write stays on your device, or — if you opt in — in your personal iCloud key-value store. Nothing is sent to a server we operate, because there isn't one.
